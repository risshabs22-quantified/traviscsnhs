Officer portraits stay in `public/officers/` and are real photos. Do not generate named officers.

The briefs below are leftovers if you want replacements. Each one says where it goes, what size to make it, and a prompt you can paste into an image generator.

## The colors to use in every prompt

Say these out loud in the prompt so everything matches:

- cream `#FBF6EE`
- tan `#F0E3D0`
- orange `#E8752A`
- light orange `#F2A03D`
- burnt red `#8E2C1B`
- dark brown `#2A1710`

And say this in every prompt: **no purple, no blue, no neon, no text, no logos, warm and soft, a little grainy like paper.**

## How to add one once you have it

1. Save it in `public/` as a `.jpg` or `.png`.
2. Use `next/image` and give it an `alt` that says what is in the picture.
3. Keep files under about 300 KB.

## Two things to keep in mind

**Faces.** Only real photos of real officers go on the Officers page. Generated people are fine as background texture on About and Events, but never as a named person.

**Skip anything that does not help.** A picture that just fills space makes the page slower and worse. The site reads fine with none of these.
